For the first task, I read data from a potentiometer, logged it onto the serial monitor and turned on the LED  when
the potentiometer voltage went over the threshold (about 70% of the maximum voltage)

For the sencond task, the LED blinked at a rate that changed when a pushbutton was pressed, and the respective blink mode was indicated on the serial monitor

I used an ESP32 board on a simulator for both tasks, and included videos for both of them
